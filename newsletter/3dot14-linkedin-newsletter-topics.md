# 3dot14 LinkedIn Newsletter — Editorial Plan (10 Issues)

**Working title:** *The Growth Ledger* by 3dot14
**Tagline:** What actually moves ROAS in mobile growth. No hype, just the numbers.
**Cadence:** Every two weeks (LinkedIn Newsletter + canonical version on 3dot14.co/insights)
**Audience:** UA managers, growth leads, performance marketers, CMOs at app companies; app publishers and developers; agency media buyers
**Editorial stance:** Data-led, slightly contrarian, written by an operator, not a vendor. Every issue carries one proprietary number from the 3dot14 DSP or Creative Studio.

## The 10 Issues

### 1. The CPI Trap: Why the Cheapest Install Is Usually the Most Expensive User
**Angle:** Teams still optimize UA to cost per install. Walk through cohort data showing how low-CPI sources under-index on D7/D30 ROAS, and how to re-brief a DSP on LTV instead of installs.
**Why it engages:** Attacks a KPI most readers report to their boss. Invites "this is us" comments and reshares by finance-minded growth leads.
**CTA:** Download the LTV-first campaign brief template.

### 2. Incrementality or It Didn't Happen
**Angle:** Attribution is a claims system, not a truth system. Explain geo holdouts, ghost ads, and always-on lift tests in plain language, with one anonymised case where "top-performing" retargeting was mostly cannibalised organics.
**Why it engages:** Contrarian, exec-relevant, and gives readers language to use with their CFO.
**CTA:** Book a 30-minute incrementality audit.

### 3. Creative Is the New Targeting
**Angle:** Post-ATT and with Android privacy changes, the audience lever has shrunk and the creative lever has grown. Show how creative testing velocity, not budget, separated the top and bottom quartile of accounts in the last quarter.
**Why it engages:** Directly showcases Creative Studio without a sales pitch. Highly visual, carousel-friendly.
**CTA:** Get the creative testing scorecard.

### 4. The Signal-Loss Playbook: Running Performance UA When Your Data Is Delayed, Aggregated, or Missing
**Angle:** A practical guide to SKAN/AdAttributionKit, Privacy Sandbox on Android, modeled conversions, and how ML bidding fills the gaps. What to measure weekly instead of daily.
**Why it engages:** Evergreen search traffic for the website, high save rate on LinkedIn.
**CTA:** Subscribe for the quarterly privacy-change tracker.

### 5. Retargeting Isn't Dead. Lazy Retargeting Is.
**Angle:** How to segment lapsed users by predicted value, when a win-back is worth paying for and when it isn't, and what frequency capping actually does to conversion. Includes the payback math.
**Why it engages:** Retargeting is a 3dot14 core product and a constant budget debate for readers.
**CTA:** Retargeting ROI calculator.

### 6. The Mobile Ad Fraud Index (Quarterly)
**Angle:** A recurring issue built from 3dot14 DSP data: invalid traffic rates by region, format, and app category; the newest patterns; what to add to your MMP rules this quarter.
**Why it engages:** Original data is the most reshared format on LinkedIn. Positions 3dot14 as an authority, not a vendor. Journalists pick these up.
**CTA:** Request a free traffic quality review.

### 7. Emerging Markets Are Not a Discount Bin
**Angle:** India, Southeast Asia, MENA, and LATAM are where the next billion installs come from, but most Western playbooks fail there. Cover localization beyond translation, payment-driven LTV differences, and creative formats that work on lower-end devices.
**Why it engages:** Leans into 3dot14's India origin as credibility. Highly shareable across regional LinkedIn communities.
**CTA:** Regional benchmark pack.

### 8. Inside the Black Box: What "AI-Powered Bidding" Actually Does With Your Money
**Angle:** Demystify ML media buying. What signals the model sees, how it learns from sparse conversions, where it fails, and the questions a growth lead should ask any DSP before trusting automation.
**Why it engages:** Transparency is rare in adtech and builds trust. Sparks debate with competitors and agencies.
**CTA:** "10 questions to ask your DSP" one-pager.

### 9. The Benchmark Issue: CTR, eCPM, CPI, and D7 ROAS by Vertical (Quarterly)
**Angle:** A clean data drop from the 3dot14 network across gaming, fintech, e-commerce, and utilities, with a short commentary on what moved and why.
**Why it engages:** The single highest-engagement format for B2B newsletters. Readers screenshot and reshare charts; it becomes the reference people link to.
**CTA:** Full benchmark report on 3dot14.co (gated for lead capture).

### 10. How to Defend Your UA Budget in the Next Board Meeting
**Angle:** Written for the CMO/CFO conversation. Payback windows, blended vs paid CAC, the cost of pausing spend, and how to present UA as a growth asset rather than a cost line. Includes a one-slide template.
**Why it engages:** Senior-audience content that gets forwarded upward. Builds 3dot14's relationships at the decision-maker level, not just the operator level.
**CTA:** Board-ready UA slide template.

## Operating Rules
1. **One proprietary number per issue.** If we cannot say something the reader cannot get elsewhere, we do not publish.
2. **Website first, LinkedIn second.** Publish the canonical piece on 3dot14.co, then the LinkedIn Newsletter version with a short intro and a link back. Protects SEO and gives LinkedIn a native read.
3. **Break every issue into five derivative posts.** One carousel, one chart, one contrarian one-liner from the founder, one poll, one employee-voice post. Schedule across the two weeks.
4. **Leadership amplifies within 60 minutes.** Founders and sales leaders comment with a point of view, not "great read".
5. **Measure the right thing.** Track subscriber growth, open and read-through rate, comments per issue, and inbound demo requests attributed to newsletter UTMs. Review monthly, cut what does not perform.
